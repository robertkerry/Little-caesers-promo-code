Little Caesars vs Domino's: Which Is Cheaper in 2026?

Little Caesars wins on a plain large pepperoni at board price in all three cities we checked. But that is not the whole answer. Domino's runs a $7.99 carryout deal on large one-topping pizzas and a $6.99 Mix & Match, and at the Chicago stores we checked, those deals flip the result. The cheaper chain depends on what you order, whether you pick it up, and which city you are in.

We checked both chains' official menus on November 26, 2026, at stores in Detroit, Dallas, and Chicago. Prices vary by store, so every figure below names the city it came from. Prices are before tax; tax is added at checkout at both chains.

Last checked: November 26, 2026

Quick Answer

Short answer: Little Caesars for one plain pizza; Domino's deals close the gap on bigger orders. For one large pepperoni with no deal applied, Little Caesars is cheaper everywhere we checked: $6.49 in Detroit, $6.99 in Dallas, and $10.99 in Chicago, against Domino's $16.74, $16.99, and $18.89 for a 14-inch hand-tossed large pepperoni. That is a gap of about $8 to $10 a pizza. The gap narrows fast once you use the deals each chain is actually running. Domino's weeklong carryout offer is $7.99 for a large one-topping pizza, carryout only. Against that, Little Caesars still wins in Detroit and Dallas by about a dollar — but in Chicago, where Little Caesars' large pepperoni is $10.99, Domino's $7.99 carryout pizza is $3.00 cheaper. On a two-pizza family order, Domino's Mix & Match ($6.99 each for two or more medium two-topping pizzas, sides, or desserts) undercuts Little Caesars' two large pizzas in Chicago, while Little Caesars wins in Detroit and Dallas — and wins by more once its TOUCHDOWN code applies.

Two things in that table are worth a second look. First, the Cheesy Garlic Pepperoni is $6.99 at all three Little Caesars stores — it is the Hot-N-Ready item in the 4–8 p.m. window, so it does not move with the store the way the Classic Pepperoni does. Second, a made-to-order large pepperoni at Little Caesars cost more than the listed Classic in Detroit ($8.74) and Dallas ($10.99), but in Chicago it was $10.36 — slightly less than the $10.99 Classic on the same menu. If you build your own pizza, check the price on your store's menu before you assume it costs more.

Domino's has no plain Cheesy Bread on its menu; Stuffed Cheesy Bread is its equivalent, which is why the table compares those two.

One large pepperoni, pickup, no deal: Little Caesars by $8 to $10

This is the simplest order there is — walk in or order online, pick up one large pepperoni, pay, leave.

  • Detroit: Little Caesars $6.49 vs Domino's $16.74 — Little Caesars wins by $10.25.
  • Dallas: Little Caesars $6.99 vs Domino's $16.99 — Little Caesars wins by $10.00.
  • Chicago: Little Caesars $10.99 vs Domino's $18.89 — Little Caesars wins by $7.90.

No code helps either side here. Little Caesars' TOUCHDOWN code (15% off orders of $15 or more) does not apply to a single pizza under $15, and Domino's board price is the price. If this is your order, Little Caesars is the answer in all three cities.

One large pepperoni, pickup, with the deals running: Domino's wins in Chicago

Same order, but this time each chain's live deal is applied.

Domino's weeklong carryout offer is $7.99 for a large one-topping pizza, carryout only. Little Caesars' standing cheap single is the Cheesy Garlic Pepperoni at $6.99 in the 4–8 p.m. Hot-N-Ready window; outside that window the Classic Pepperoni is the price.

  • Detroit: Little Caesars $6.49 vs Domino's $7.99 — Little Caesars still wins, by $1.50.
  • Dallas: Little Caesars $6.99 vs Domino's $7.99 — Little Caesars still wins, by $1.00.
  • Chicago: Little Caesars $10.99 vs Domino's $7.99 — Domino's wins, by $3.00.

This is the order where the "which is cheaper" answer changes with the city. In Chicago, the carryout deal is the whole ballgame. The $7.99 offer is a limited-time weeklong deal, so it can end — if it is gone, the board-price answer above is the one that applies.

Two pizzas and a bread side, pickup: Little Caesars in Detroit and Dallas, Domino's Mix & Match in Chicago

A family order: two pizzas and a cheesy bread side, picked up.

Little Caesars: two large Classic Pepperonis plus Crazy Bread.

  • Detroit: $6.49 + $6.49 + $3.49 = $16.47.
  • Dallas: $6.99 + $6.99 + $3.99 = $17.97.
  • Chicago: $10.99 + $10.99 + $5.99 = $27.97.

Domino's Mix & Match: two medium two-topping pizzas at $6.99 each plus Stuffed Cheesy Bread.

  • Detroit: $6.99 + $6.99 + $7.99 = $21.97.
  • Dallas: $6.99 + $6.99 + $8.99 = $22.97.
  • Chicago: $6.99 + $6.99 + $9.99 = $23.97.

Two honest notes. First, the sizes are not the same: Mix & Match pizzas are medium, Little Caesars' are large, so the Little Caesars order is more pizza. In Detroit and Dallas, Little Caesars gives you more pizza for less money. In Chicago, Domino's Mix & Match is cheaper on the bill ($23.97 vs $27.97) but you take home mediums.

Second, the family order is big enough for Little Caesars' TOUCHDOWN code (15% off orders of $15 or more, $5 maximum savings, ends February 14, 2027). With it, the Little Caesars totals become $14.00 in Detroit, $15.27 in Dallas, and $23.77 in Chicago. TOUCHDOWN rotates on and off the deals page, so check whether it is listed today — we track its current status on our homepage, where the live codes are listed.

What about delivery?

We do not print a delivery total here, because neither chain showed one. Both chains' delivery checkout needs a full street address, and neither menu shows a delivery fee on a pickup order. Delivery adds a fee that varies by store and address, on top of the food prices above. If you are ordering delivery from Little Caesars, the DELIVERY20 code (20% off a delivery order of $25 or more, $5 maximum savings, ends December 27, 2026) is the one that cuts the bill — a $25-plus delivery order is exactly the size where it applies.

When does Domino's beat Little Caesars on price?

  • One large pepperoni in Chicago, with the $7.99 carryout deal running — $7.99 against $10.99.
  • A two-pizza family order in Chicago, through Mix & Match — $23.97 against $27.97, keeping in mind the Domino's pizzas are mediums.
  • A custom multi-topping order, where Domino's deal structure does the work: Mix & Match is built for two-topping pizzas and bundles, while a made-to-order large at Little Caesars cost more than its listed Classic in Detroit and Dallas.

Everywhere else in our three-city check — a plain large pepperoni, and a family order in Detroit or Dallas — Little Caesars is cheaper, and TOUCHDOWN stretches the lead on orders over $15.

Which tastes better?

On taste, the videos we found call it close. A car taste-test video with about 20,000 likes scored Little Caesars' cheese pizza 7.5 out of 10 and its cheese sticks 6 out of 10, and the top comments kept coming back to the price — "Think of that price tho" and "always been there when we was broke." A "Fast Food Wars" audience tournament came down to a Domino's vs Little Caesars final. Nobody in that evidence calls either chain a clear taste winner, so taste is not the decider here. The prices above are.

The bottom line

Order from Little Caesars if you want one plain large pepperoni for pickup, or a two-large family order in Detroit or Dallas — and use TOUCHDOWN on any order over $15 while it is live. Order from Domino's if you are in Chicago and the $7.99 carryout deal is running, or if a Mix & Match bundle is what your order looks like anyway. Before you order either way, check two things: your own store's price, because the same pizza was $6.49 in Detroit and $10.99 in Chicago, and whether Domino's $7.99 carryout deal is still on — those two checks decide the answer more than the brand does.
