How much is a Little Caesars Hot-N-Ready pizza in 2026?

The Hot-N-Ready pizza most people mean costs $6.99 in 2026. The Cheesy Garlic Pepperoni and the Cheesy Garlic Cheese are both $6.99 at the Detroit, Dallas, and Chicago stores we checked. The large Classic Pepperoni, the other pizza people call Hot-N-Ready, is $6.49 in Detroit, $6.99 in Dallas, and $10.99 in Chicago.

The 4 to 8 p.m. window appears on the Little Caesars homepage banner for the Cheesy Garlic Pepperoni. The store menus we read showed no time window at all, so the $6.99 price is what the menu lists, and the window is what the homepage advertises.

The $5 Hot-N-Ready is history. A September 2026 menu-history piece reports the $5 flat price held for about 25 years, from 2001 to 2022, before the first increase to $5.55 in January 2022. Our three-store check found a wider range than the trackers suggest, up to $10.99 in Chicago, so treat tracker ranges as a starting point, not a ceiling.

Why is the same pizza a different price at different stores?

Little Caesars stores are franchises, and each franchise sets its own menu prices. That is why the same large Classic Pepperoni is $6.49 in Detroit and $10.99 in Chicago, and why some items appear on one store's menu and not another's.

Chicago was the most expensive of the three stores on almost every item we compared, and Detroit was the cheapest. The gap is biggest on the Classic Pepperoni, $4.50 between Detroit and Chicago, and smallest on the Cheesy Garlic Pepperoni, which is $6.99 everywhere.

The menus are not identical either:

  • Webberoni is listed in Dallas and Chicago but not in Detroit.
  • Detroit-Style Deep Dish Sausage is listed in Dallas and Chicago but not in Detroit.
  • Chicago's Deep Dish menu lists only three pizzas: cheese, pepperoni, and sausage.

Before you order, open your own store's menu in the app or on the website. A price from another city, or from a price-tracking site, can be dollars off what your store charges.

Are DoorDash prices higher than the Little Caesars app?

Mostly yes, and by a fixed percentage per city. In Detroit the DoorDash menu prices we checked run about 31 percent higher than the app, in Dallas about 21 percent, and in Chicago about 25 percent. Two items in Chicago cost exactly the same on both: Crazy Bread and Pepperoni Crazy Puffs.

The cheapest pizza on the app is not on DoorDash at all. None of the three DoorDash menus lists a Classic Pepperoni or Classic Cheese. The closest plain large pepperoni there is the ExtraMostBestest Pepperoni: $9.79 in Detroit, $9.64 in Dallas, and $14.99 in Chicago, against $6.49, $6.99, and $10.99 for the Classic on the app.

What is on the More for $9.99 menu?

The More for $9.99 menu is a set of pizza-plus-side combinations that all cost $9.99 at all three stores we checked. It is the one part of the menu where the price does not move by city.

  • Cheesy Garlic Pepperoni with Crazy Bread
  • Cheesy Garlic Cheese with Crazy Bread
  • A large Classic pizza with Crazy Puffs
  • Slices-N-Stix with Crazy Bread
  • Slices-N-Stix with a 2 liter
  • Wings with Crazy Bread
  • Pretzel Crust Combo

The $9.99 price is the same in all three cities, which makes it the easiest way to predict your total before you order.

A $9.99 order is below the $15 minimum for the TOUCHDOWN code, so no code applies to it. On a $15 order, TOUCHDOWN cuts 15 percent, capped at $5, as long as the deals page still lists it. You can see whether it is listed today on the current Little Caesars promo codes page.
