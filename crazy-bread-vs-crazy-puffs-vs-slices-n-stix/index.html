Crazy Bread vs Crazy Puffs vs Slices-N-Stix: Which Little Caesars Side Is Worth It?

There is no single winner. Crazy Bread is the cheapest bite at 44 to 75 cents a stick, Pepperoni Crazy Puffs are the priciest bite at $1.00 to $1.25 a puff, and Slices-N-Stix is the only one that can replace a second pizza.

Last checked: November 26, 2026

Quick Answer

There is no single winner. Crazy Bread is the cheapest bite at 44 to 75 cents a stick, Pepperoni Crazy Puffs are the priciest bite at $1.00 to $1.25 a puff, and Slices-N-Stix is the only one that can replace a second pizza.

All three prices come from the official ordering menu, read on November 26, 2026, and all of them are before tax. Every calorie figure comes from Little Caesars' own nutrition guide.

What is in each box?

The three sides are built differently, and that decides what each one is good for.

Crazy Bread is eight garlic-Parmesan breadsticks. Pepperoni Crazy Puffs are four filled puffs, pepperoni and cheese baked inside each one. Slices-N-Stix is a hybrid box: four pepperoni pizza slices, eight cheese stix, and a cup of Crazy Sauce.

SideWhat is insideCalories (whole order)
Crazy Bread8 breadsticks800 (100 per stick)
Pepperoni Crazy Puffs4 filled puffs680 (170 per puff)
Slices-N-Stix4 pepperoni slices, 8 cheese stix, Crazy Sauce2,300

The calorie figures are Little Caesars' own numbers for the whole order. The per-piece figures are simple division: 800 divided by 8 sticks, 680 divided by 4 puffs. The Slices-N-Stix box has about the same calories as a whole large Classic Pepperoni pizza, so it is a meal, not a snack.

What does each bite cost?

The menu price hides the real difference, because the boxes hold different amounts of food. Two numbers show it: the price per bite, and the price per 100 calories.

Which side should you get?

It depends on what the side has to do. Three situations, three picks.

  • Eating alone, no pizza. Pepperoni Crazy Puffs. Four filled puffs are a snack on their own, and you do not need a pizza beside them. $3.99 in Detroit and Dallas, $4.99 in Chicago.
  • Sharing with a Hot-N-Ready pizza. Crazy Bread. Eight sticks split easily, and at 44 to 75 cents a stick it is the cheapest thing on the table. $3.49 in Detroit, $3.99 in Dallas, $5.99 in Chicago.
  • Feeding two people without a second pizza. Slices-N-Stix. Four slices plus eight stix is a meal for two, and at $7.99 in Detroit and Dallas it costs less than a second large pizza. In Chicago it is $12.99, so check the bundle below first.

Is there a cheaper way to get Slices-N-Stix?

Yes, and in Chicago the saving is big. The More for $9.99 menu includes Slices-N-Stix with Crazy Bread for $9.99 at all three stores.

Bought separately, the two cost $11.48 in Detroit, $11.98 in Dallas, and $18.98 in Chicago. The bundle saves $1.49 in Detroit, $1.99 in Dallas, and $8.99 in Chicago.

The bundle is the cheapest way to get Slices-N-Stix anywhere, and in Chicago it is the only way the box makes sense against a $12.99 menu price. If the pizza is the main order, the Little Caesars codes that are live today can cut that part of the total; the side itself is full price either way.
