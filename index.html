Why Isn't My Little Caesars Promo Code Working?

  • The code expired. Many run for a specific week or even specific days of the week.
  • Wrong channel. App-only or web-only codes won't apply in-store or on third-party delivery sites.
  • Your store isn't participating. Franchise owners can opt out of a given national promotion.
  • Minimum spend not met. Some codes require a specific subtotal or item, like a particular pizza type.
  • Already used. Most codes allow one redemption per person, account, or day.
  • Conflicting discount. An app deal already applied to your cart often blocks a code from stacking on top.
  • Regional restriction. A code circulating online may apply in Canada but not the United States, or to a specific local market.
