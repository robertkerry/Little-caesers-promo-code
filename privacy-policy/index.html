Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Short Version

This site does not require an account, does not use cookies or analytics trackers, and does not use affiliate links. The only personal data we handle is what you choose to send us if you email us directly.

What We Collect

Browsing this site doesn't require you to create an account or submit personal information. If you email us through the address on our contact page, we'll have whatever information you include in that email, such as your email address and message content, and we use it only to respond to you.

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes in the future, for example if analytics software is added, this page will be updated to describe what's collected and why before that change goes live.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard server information, such as IP address and browser type, as part of operating its hosting infrastructure. That logging is governed by GitHub's own privacy practices, not by us directly.

Third-Party Sites

Links to Little Caesars' own site and app, and to third-party retailers, delivery platforms, or gift card marketplaces, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies once you leave littlecaesarscoupons.us. We don't control and aren't responsible for how they handle your data.

Children's Privacy

This site isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, for example if we add analytics, a newsletter signup, or advertising, we'll update this page to reflect that before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
