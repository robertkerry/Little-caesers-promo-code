Little Caesars Military Discount: What You Actually Get (July 2026)

Little Caesars' military discount isn't a year-round percentage off your order. It's a single, well-established Veterans Day tradition. Every November 11, participating locations hand veterans and active-duty service members a free HOT-N-READY Lunch Combo: four slices of Detroit-Style Deep Dish pizza and a 20-oz. Pepsi product, no purchase necessary, no coupon required. That's the real, confirmed offer. Everything else floating around, like claims of "25% off every visit" or "verify through SheerID for ongoing savings," traces back to coupon-aggregator sites, not to Little Caesars itself, and doesn't hold up when you check the company's own announcements.

This page breaks down what's actually confirmed, what to bring with you, how Little Caesars stacks up against other pizza chains on Veterans Day, and where the year-round discount claims fall apart.

Last checked: July 25, 2026

The Free HOT-N-READY Lunch Combo, Explained

Little Caesars co-founder Mike Ilitch served in the U.S. Army, and that experience led the company to launch a dedicated veterans program in 2006, not a coupon promotion, but a franchise initiative offering honorably discharged veterans discounted franchise fees, equipment and supply discounts, and financing help to open their own Little Caesars locations. That program has grown into a network of hundreds of veteran-owned stores nationwide. The free Veterans Day lunch grew out of that same relationship with the veteran community and has run annually alongside the franchise program for close to two decades.

That history matters for one practical reason: it's why the Veterans Day offer keeps returning every year without fail, even though Little Caesars doesn't run a broader everyday military discount.

When and How to Claim the Veterans Day Offer

  • Date: November 11 only, at participating locations.
  • Time window: roughly late morning through early afternoon at most stores.
  • How to claim it: walk in and ask for the free Lunch Combo. No app code, no online form, no advance registration; you just show proof of service at the register.
  • Guests: anyone dining with you, including family or friends, pays for their own food unless they're also veterans or active-duty themselves.
  • Before you go: hours and participation can shift slightly from year to year and store to store, so if you're planning to stop by later in the day, call your local location that morning to confirm they're running it and check the cutoff time.

Proof of Service Little Caesars Accepts at Checkout

Based on how Little Caesars has run the offer in past years, the store tends to be fairly flexible about what counts as proof:

Acceptable Proof of Service
Active-duty U.S. uniformed services ID card
Retired uniformed services ID card
Veterans ID card
DD-214 discharge form (a printed copy from the DoD eBenefits site works if you don't have the original)
Leave and Earnings Statement (LES)
ID card from a veterans organization, such as the American Legion or VFW
State-issued driver's license or ID with a veteran designation

If you're unsure whether what you're carrying will count, it's worth calling ahead rather than finding out at the register during a lunch rush.

Does Little Caesars Offer a Year-Round Military Discount?

Not one the company has confirmed. Search around and you'll find coupon sites claiming a standing 10 to 25 percent military discount tied to services like SheerID or ID.me verification. None of that shows up on Little Caesars' own site, its app, or in any official company statement, and the wording on those pages tends to read like generic templated coupon-site copy rather than anything specific to this brand. Treat those claims the way you'd treat any unverified coupon listing: possibly true at an individual franchise's discretion, but not something to expect walking in. A similar pattern shows up around claims of a standing Little Caesars student discount, which we also couldn't confirm.

What is real, separate from Veterans Day, is the ongoing veteran franchise support program described above, but that's aimed at veterans opening a store, not customers buying a pizza.

Common Reasons the Offer Doesn't Go Smoothly

  • Wrong day or window. The offer is Veterans Day only, and past years ran it during a set midday window rather than all day, so it's not a dinner deal.
  • Non-participating location. Corporate-run stores almost always honor it, but a small number of franchise locations may handle it differently. A quick call avoids a wasted trip.
  • Unclear proof of service. Bring one of the accepted ID types listed above rather than assuming any veteran-related paperwork will work.
  • Expecting it to stack. It's one combo per verified person, not a discount code, so it generally can't be combined with app deals or promo codes on the same order.

Final Thoughts

If someone mentions a Little Caesars military discount, they're almost certainly talking about the free Veterans Day Lunch Combo, a real, ID-verified offer with no coupon needed that's been running for close to twenty years. Claims of a bigger, everyday percentage-off discount aren't backed by anything Little Caesars has actually said, so it's worth staying skeptical of them until the offer shows up in the app or on the official site itself. Want to treat a veteran or service member outside of November 11? A Little Caesars gift card works any day of the year.
