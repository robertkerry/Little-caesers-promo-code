Denominations and Formats

Physical Little Caesars gift cards are most commonly found in $5, $10, and $15 amounts at grocery and retail chains, though $25 physical and digital versions also show up through online retailers. There is no single official price list published on littlecaesars.com, so the safest approach is to check the specific retailer's listing before buying.

Digital eGift cards work the same way as physical ones once redeemed. They're delivered by email or text, which makes them a practical option if you're buying a same-day gift or ordering for someone in another city.

How to Check Your Little Caesars Gift Card Balance

There are four ways to check a balance, and which one works best depends on whether you have the physical card in hand:

  • Call the number on the back of the card. The terms printed on current cards list 1-888-892-3813 for balance and card-related inquiries. Some older cards or third-party listings reference 1-800-722-3727, so if one number doesn't connect, try the one actually printed on your card.
  • Visit littlecaesars.com and use the gift card section, entering the card number and the security code from the back.
  • Check inside the Little Caesars app if the card has already been added to your account.
  • Ask a cashier in-store. This is the most reliable option if you're near a location anyway, since staff can look up the balance directly at the register.

How to Redeem a Little Caesars Gift Card

In-store

  1. Order at the counter or kiosk as usual.
  2. Tell the cashier you're paying with a gift card before the transaction closes.
  3. Hand over the card to be swiped or scanned.
  4. Ask for a receipt showing the remaining balance, since not every card prints this automatically.

In the app or online

  1. Open the Little Caesars app and go to your account or payment methods.
  2. Add the gift card using the card number and the security code on the back.
  3. At checkout, select the gift card as your payment method for pickup or delivery orders.
  4. Any remaining balance stays on the card for the next order.

Little Caesars gift cards are redeemable for food and beverage only at participating U.S. locations. They are not redeemable for cash except where state law requires it, and lost or stolen cards are not replaced.

Corporate and Bulk Gift Cards

Little Caesars gift cards are commonly used for employee incentives, client gifts, and event giveaways, and bulk purchase options exist for businesses ordering larger quantities. If you're ordering for a company or organization, the most reliable route is to contact Little Caesars directly through their corporate or gift card inquiry line rather than assuming a retail store can fulfill a large order, since in-store stock is meant for individual customers.

Terms and Exclusions Worth Knowing

  • Cards are not valid until activated by a cashier at purchase.
  • Redeemable for food or beverage only at participating U.S. Little Caesars locations.
  • Not redeemable or refundable for cash, except where required by law.
  • Lost, stolen, or damaged cards are not replaced.
  • Cards resold through unauthorized channels, such as unverified online auctions, can be voided.
  • Little Caesar Enterprises, Inc. is the card issuer, though it can delegate that role to an assignee without notice.

Why a Gift Card Might Not Work

A card can fail to scan or ring up correctly for a few common reasons: it was never activated at the time of purchase, the balance was already spent, the card was purchased through an unauthorized reseller and later voided, or the store's system is having a temporary card-reader issue. If a card won't process, ask the cashier to manually check the balance before assuming the card itself is invalid.
